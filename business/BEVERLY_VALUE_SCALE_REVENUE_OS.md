# Beverly & Sons — Value & Scale Revenue OS v1.0
Status: Draft operating playbook, owner approval required for external actions
Budget: $0 organic
Framework: Inspired by value investing and operational efficiency; no endorsement or affiliation with Buffett, Berkshire Hathaway, Rockefeller, or Standard Oil.

## North star
Create valuable original IP once, distribute it responsibly, fulfill reliably, build consent-based customer relationships, and measure verified net contribution.

## Value lens (Buffett-inspired)
1. Intrinsic value: Show actual features, audience fit, and evidence-backed benefits; never invent time savings, returns, testimonials, or outcomes.
2. Margin of safety: Conservative / base / optimistic scenarios are explicit assumptions, not guarantees.
3. Circle of competence: Qualify audiences by genuine use case, and explain who should not buy.
4. Long-term compounding: Reuse approved source IP across books, lessons, podcasts, and videos while tracking rights and edition control.
5. Quality: No sale until source, editing, PDF/EPUB, cover, checkout, delivery, and refund support pass QA.

## Scale lens (Rockefeller-inspired)
Source IP -> editorial and rights approval -> reusable campaign assets -> approved organic distribution -> tracked landing page -> verified checkout -> digital fulfillment -> opt-in follow-up -> verified analytics -> improvement.
Vertical integration means operational ownership, not monopoly behavior, spam, or bypassing platform rules.

## Sales math (illustrative, not forecast)
Gross receipts = paid units * actual unit price.
Net contribution = gross receipts - refunds - taxes collected - processor fees - fulfillment costs - attributable expenses.
Conversion rate = verified purchases / measured qualified landing-page sessions.
LTV:CAC can be computed only after real cohort and acquisition cost data exists.
A 30/70/100% sensitivity model is illustrative, not customer ROI evidence.
