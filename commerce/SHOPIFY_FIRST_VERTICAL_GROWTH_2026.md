## Priority sequence
1. **Fulfillment readiness**: Audit active product file attachments, payment checkout, tax and customer receipt/download flows; do not drive paid traffic to products with missing files. Keep unpublished/unready products in draft until tested and approved.
2. **One conversion path**: CAAVE Daily Keys free art and daily lesson -> SEO/share link -> relevant Shopify product -> verified checkout -> Digital Products delivery -> consented follow-up. Track campaign UTMs and conversion by landing page.
3. **High-fit niches to test**: original family/youth education, printable coloring and activities, parent-child learning, identity/legacy workbooks, creator-owned comic IP, educational music tie-ins. These are hypotheses, not independently measured 2026 market-size rankings.
4. **Merchandising**: curated collections and bundles only when all files and product descriptions exist; preserve approved prices. Avoid duplicate listings and unfinished generated products.
5. **Content engine**: source IP -> approved art/lesson -> 6 channel adaptations (YouTube, TikTok, IG, FB, Pinterest, LinkedIn) -> one tracked product CTA. Reuse each source as a long article, short, coloring sample, and educational email with distinct value.
6. **Metrics**: weekly sessions, add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, paid orders, revenue, average order value, refund rate, delivery failures, email signups and repeat purchase. Prioritize first 100 qualified visits and first 10 fulfilled orders over arbitrary product count.
7. **Operational controls**: privacy, opt-in email, rights, child-directed content safeguards, copyright provenance, truthful claims and advertising disclosures. Never invent view/revenue figures or pay for unverified attention events.
8. **Architecture**: Shopify = catalog/checkout/order source of truth. Digital Products = delivery. Daily Keys = attention/SEO. Content Factory v6 = draft products. Master OS v8 = draft orchestrator. Sovereign v2 = ledger design. GitHub = code/IP registry. Supabase = optional user library and editorial queue. Stripe direct checkout must not bypass Shopify order reconciliation. Social posting remains review-gated until authorized and connected.

## 30-day milestones
Week 1: audit deliverables/checkout; correct titles, SEO and product descriptions; publish 3 fully delivered hero products.
Week 2: launch one Key per day and 2-3 platform-specific posts/day with tracked links; build opt-in free lesson/worksheet.
Week 3: launch 1 validated Key coloring volume and one bundle after QA; start email welcome sequence.
Week 4: inspect traffic sources and funnel; iterate strongest offers; only consider paid ads after verified delivery and a conversion baseline.
