## Governance requirements
- Never generate covert manipulation, emotional dependency marketing, impersonation or misleading urgency.
- Do not present the service as therapy or diagnose users. Escalate mental health emergencies to appropriate help.
- Privacy-by-design for sensitive behavioral profiles; opt-in, data minimization, deletion, access controls and retention policy.
- Evidence grades and source citations for strategic/psychological claims; mark unverified assertions.
- RAG only from properly licensed or authorized materials. Do not reproduce copyrighted books as an unlicensed corpus.
- Separate education from individualized regulated legal, mental-health and investment services.
- Human review before publishing or monetizing a lesson; accessible corrections and appeals.
- No live automations, model inference, commerce, or deployment are enabled by this document.
